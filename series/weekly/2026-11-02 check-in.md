## Your turn: writing and research prompt

Write this one yourself. I won't draft it.

**Write:**
- Think of the first time you found a word for something that had already happened to you.
- What were you calling it before you had the word?
- Who did you say the new word to first, and what changed after you said it out loud?
- Leave out names, dates, and places from work.

**Research:**
- Pick one word you lean on now, such as "gaslighting," "masking," or "neurodiversity."
- Find out when it first showed up in print or in a dictionary, and what people said before it existed.
- Note your source and its date, and bring it back. I'll check it before it goes in a post.
