# Evidence snapshot: WBI 2024 U.S. Workplace Bullying Survey

Source: https://workplacebullying.org/2024-wbi-us-survey/ (Key Findings section). Retrieved 2026-10-24.
These are short factual lines copied from the page so the offline checker can match figures. They are not the full page.

- Directly bullied – 32.3% of adult Americans
- Extrapolated to those working – 52.2 million workers
- 72% are aware of workplace bullying
- Affected by bullying – sum of direct + indirect – 74.8 million
- 75% said bully acted alone
- 29% of perpetrators were peers of equal rank
- "bully boss" still represents the most frequent rank, 55%
- Targets have a 62% of losing their jobs after targeting
- Perpetrators suffered negative consequences ... (27% vs. 62%)
- Coworkers remain a disappointment with a dismal rate of intervention and support for targets (2%)
- Strong & somewhat strong support (87%) crossed ideological boundaries
