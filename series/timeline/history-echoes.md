# History echoes: connecting "On the Bus or Off the Bus" to the series

**Timeline:** [On the Bus or Off the Bus](https://claude.ai/artifact/U8XhxLs1m2Xs8o4ydrXA8k), a ledger of 1601–2026 in six lanes (measure, remove, cut, kill, speak, frame).

**How the two projects connect.** The series names the tactics a person meets at work today. The timeline shows that the same moves were built into institutions long before: someone gets measured, labeled, moved out of sight, and then disbelieved when they speak. Each weekly batch can now carry one **history echo**, a single slide or post that pairs a present-day tactic with one entry from the timeline and links to it.

**Rules for echoes**
- Use the timeline's own wording and dates. It was fact-checked when it was built, so don't add new claims on the slide. If a caption needs more, cite the source from that entry.
- An echo shows a pattern. It doesn't say any employer is doing what a historical institution did. Say "the same move," not "the same thing."
- Leave the killing-era entries (T4, Spiegelgrund) out of quick social posts. They belong in long-form Substack pieces, where there's room for care and context.
- Every echo ends with the timeline link or "full timeline in bio."
