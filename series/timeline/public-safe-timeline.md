## The arc in one paragraph (pattern-safe)

I worked there for years and was good at my job. One task triggered a disability-related reaction, and I kept getting pushed toward it. One day it led to a meltdown on the floor in front of coworkers, and some of them said I didn't "look autistic." My doctor filled out accommodation paperwork that same day, and it was approved. Later I got a formal autism diagnosis, and after that the way I was spoken to changed. Within weeks I was praised as a top performer, and then I was written up. I asked for help in writing. More praise came, and then more discipline. I reported what was happening. Then I went on leave. Everywhere else in my life was healing. Work wasn't.
